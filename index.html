Travel Tips

Here are some important tips before travelling:

1. Carry your passport and travel documents.
2. Plan your budget before travelling.
3. Book your accommodation in advance.
4. Respect local cultures and traditions.
5. Keep your belongings safe.

Travel safely and enjoy every moment!
